# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for every team member and contributor.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Give credit to contributors.
- Welcome different technical opinions and backgrounds.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Protect confidential information and credentials.
- Ask for clarification instead of making assumptions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption of the repository or team workflow.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or presenting another person's work as your own.
- Committing secrets, credentials, or unsafe operational guidance.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers or project leads. Include enough context to investigate, but do not post sensitive information publicly.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request corrective action, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the team.
